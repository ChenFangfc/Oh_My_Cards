# Storyboard

32 s at 120 BPM (a bar is 2 s). One idea per beat: the problem, the answer in
one line, the app behind it, and why it can be trusted with a wallet.

| Time | Picture | Words (en / zh) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.4 | Five cards rise into a neat stack, each printed with its reward rule (5× flights, 3× dining, 5× top category, 1.5% everything, 3% gas). | Five cards in your wallet. / 钱包里有五张卡。 |
| 2.0–4.5 | They fan out like a hand; the "5× top category" card lifts, "5× on dinner", the others fall out of focus. | Which one pays you most? / 这一笔，刷哪张最划算？ |
| 4.5–5.5 | That card flies to the menu bar and becomes its icon. | — |
| 5.5–11 | The pointer clicks the icon; the panel opens; "45 dinner at nopa" is typed and ranked; the best card is ringed and logged, +$2.25. | Type what you bought. / It picks the card that pays the most. · 写下买了什么，/ 自动选出回报最高的卡。 |
| 11–16 | The panel becomes the app window. Smart Suggest: a $1,200 flight, Amex Platinum at 5×; then the better cards you don't hold. | Check before you pay. / Even against cards you don't have yet. · 付款之前，先问一句。/ 连还没办的卡也一起比。 |
| 16–20 | The wallet: what's coming up, a payment due in 3 days, an annual fee being earned back. | Due dates. Annual fees. Credits. / Nothing slips through. · 还款日、年费、报销额度，/ 一样都不会漏。 |
| 20–23 | Activity for the quarter; the card library scrolling. | Every purchase, every reward. / 180+ cards, rules kept current. |
| 23–27 | The window folds into a laptop; a lock closes. | No account. No bank login. / Your data stays on your device. · 不用注册，不连银行。/ 数据只留在你自己的设备上。 |
| 27–32 | The icon, the name sliding out from behind it, then a fade. | Oh My Cards · Free · macOS & web · English & Chinese (zh: 中文 / English) · github.com/ChenFangfc/Oh_My_Cards |

Every number on screen is computed by the app from the demo wallet in
`scripts/demo-data.mts` (two months of everyday purchases, each on the card the
app ranks first). Style after the AutoConference films: soft studio light, one
object above one line of type, an accent colour for the word that matters, real
screens in a drawn window with a ring on what to look at.
